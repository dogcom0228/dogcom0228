I am a software engineer and applied machine learning researcher working at the intersection of production systems and modern AI. My engineering philosophy is anchored in **SOLID principles**, **high cohesion with low coupling**, and architectures that remain maintainable from first commit to enterprise load. ## Professional Focus

### Software Engineering — 2+ Years in Production

Design and delivery of secure, reliable, and horizontally scalable enterprise systems. I architect backend services in **Laravel / PHP** with strong emphasis on data integrity, auditability, and long-term maintainability, paired with **Vue 3** and **Inertia.js (SSR)** frontends optimized for performance and SEO. End-to-end ownership — from schema design and API contracts through CI/CD pipelines to production observability.

### AI / ML Research — NTUST

My thesis investigates an agentic **Coding Agent** architecture designed around three guiding principles:

- **Heterogeneous Compression** — applying information-density-aware compression strategies across distinct context segments, rather than uniform truncation.
- **Phase-Aware Optimization** — differentiating agent behavior across the software engineering lifecycle: requirement understanding, diagnosis, design, implementation, and testing.
- **Trajectory Topology** — deep error provenance for root-cause identification, avoiding surface-level patches that accrue technical debt.

Adjacent research interests include **Curriculum Learning**, **Data Selection**, **Knowledge Distillation**, **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE / MoD / MoR)**, **PagedAttention**, and **FlashAttention**.
